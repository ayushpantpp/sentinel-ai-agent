# Aircraft delivery escalation

When an aircraft delivery forecast moves by more than seven calendar days, the programme manager must open a delivery-risk review within one business day. The review must include the aircraft MSN and tail number, airline owner, original and forecast delivery dates, blocking station, delay reason, and recovery owner.

Notify the airline only after the programme manager approves the customer-facing wording. A supplier delay must not be described as resolved until the affected component has passed incoming inspection and the revised production plan is accepted.

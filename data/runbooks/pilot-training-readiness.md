# Pilot training readiness

An aircraft handover readiness review must identify airline pilots whose applicable type or differences training is overdue or due within thirty days. Overdue training is a readiness blocker. Due-soon training requires a confirmed course date before entry into service.

The response should state the pilot, airline, aircraft family, course, due date, and readiness impact. Training records are operational readiness evidence and do not change the aircraft's manufacturing status.
